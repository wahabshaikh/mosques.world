Phases 9 to 12 · after Global & open

Every mosque is already a meetup. Let people find each other there.

TripBFF and NomadTable help travellers meet people who are in the same place on the same dates. PraySap lets people start a prayer anywhere and see who is going. mosques.world already knows the place and the jamā'ah time, so the social layer starts with one question: who is praying here, and when?

What we take from each reference

Three apps, three mechanics. The board keeps the mechanic and drops the party. Every social object on mosques.world hangs off a place and a prayer time the community already verifies.

tripbff.com

Same place, same dates

  • Add a trip, see who else is there on overlapping dates
  • "Trending trips" rail by city and date range
  • Groups started by one person, joined by many

Our take: Trips (phase 10). A traveller adds Istanbul, Oct 3 to 8 and sees Jumu'ah options, gatherings and Muslims overlapping their dates.

nomadtable.app

Join something today

  • Browse nearby activities, join one, chat to finalise
  • "N going" is the whole social proof
  • Businesses pay to show listings to travellers in their city

Our take: Gathering chat (phase 11) and featured listings for hosts (phase 12), which is where the first revenue comes from.

praysap.com

Pray together, anywhere

  • Prayers near you marked Live or Upcoming, with attending counts
  • Host a prayer in a mosque, home, park or office
  • Chips like Imam, Wudu, Women space, Bring mat

Our take: Pray together (phase 9). "I'm going" on every iqamah, and hosted prayers in spaces that are not mosques.

Prayer firstThe unit is a jamā'ah at a time. Activities come last and always sit next to a salah time.
Audience on everythingEvery gathering is Everyone, Brothers, Sisters or Families. Sisters-only stays sisters-only in lists, chat and attendee avatars.
No live locationWe show "in Istanbul Oct 3 to 8", never a dot on a map. Home addresses go only to people the host approves.
Trust-gatedHosting and messaging need trust level 1 or more. Report and block sit one tap away on every person and chat.
Salam, not swipePeople connect after praying together or through a salam request with context. No profile browsing grid, no photos required.
Core stays freeFinding, going, hosting and chatting are free forever. Money comes from hosts who promote and supporters who opt in.

9

Pray together

Turns every iqamah into something you can say you're going to, and lets anyone host a prayer where there's no mosque. This is the PraySap loop built on data we already have.

Flag phase9.togetherNew tab: Together
Flow F9Open mosque page→I'm going (sign in if needed)→Reminder 20 min before iqamah→"I'm here" check-in→Counts toward "prayed together"

Scope

  • "I'm going" on the next iqamah and on each Jumu'ah jamā'ah; the implicit gathering row is created lazily per place, prayer and date
  • Hosted prayers at a mosque or a custom space (park, office, home, campus)
  • Audience: Everyone, Brothers, Sisters, Families; self-declared on the profile and needed only for single-gender gatherings
  • Together tab with Now, Today, This week; card on Explore and city pages
  • Web push reminder (reuses phase 6 push) and ICS link (reuses phase 7)
  • Report, cancel and host removal of a member

Data & routes

gathering: id, place_id or custom name + coarse lat/lng, prayer, starts_at (UTC) + timezone, audience, capacity, host_user_id, kind (jamaah or hosted), note, status.

gathering_member: gathering_id, user_id, status (going, waitlist, requested, approved, left).

user.audience_group nullable column. API: /api/v1/gatherings (bbox + time), /api/v1/gatherings/[id]/members.

Measure & accept

Goals: going_tapped, gathering_created, gathering_joined, gathering_shared.

  1. Tapping I'm going on a mosque's Isha shows the user in the going list and the count goes up by one
  2. A brother can't see or join a sisters-only gathering, and its members aren't listed to him
  3. A home gathering hides the address until the host approves
10

Trips

The TripBFF mechanic, with a halal twist: a trip tells you where Jumu'ah is on your Friday, which gatherings fall on your dates, and who else is in town.

Flag phase10.tripsGrowth loop: shared trip pages
Flow F10Add trip→See Jumu'ah on your Friday→Going→Browse travellers→Send salam→Accepted: chat opens (phase 11)

Scope

  • Trips on the Together tab and on profiles (optional)
  • Trip page: Jumu'ah pick, gatherings on your dates, saved mosques in the city
  • City pages gain "Travelling here this month" with a count, which is also SEO copy
  • Salam request with a reason; accept, ignore, block
  • Until phase 11 ships, an accepted salam reveals each other's profile only
  • Email digest the day before arrival: Jumu'ah time plus three gatherings

Data & routes

trip: id, user_id, city_id, starts_on, ends_on (local dates), show_to_travellers, show_on_profile.

salam_request: from_user, to_user, trip_id, reason, message, status, created_at. Unique on (from, to, trip).

user_block: blocker, blocked. It's checked on every social read from this phase on.

Measure & accept

Goals: trip_added, trip_shared, salam_sent, salam_accepted.

  1. Two users with overlapping Istanbul trips see each other; a third with non-overlapping dates does not
  2. A blocked user disappears from traveller lists both ways
  3. Hiding a trip from travellers removes it within one request
11

Companions

NomadTable's group chat, scoped to a gathering and time-boxed, then a lightweight way to keep the people you prayed with. The profile map becomes a shared map.

Flag phase11.companionsNew tab badge: Inbox
Flow F11Join gathering→Group chat opens→Pray, check in→Add companions (mutual)→Shared map + direct messages

Scope

  • Gathering chat for members, open from 24h before to 24h after
  • Companions: mutual add after a shared check-in, or after an accepted salam
  • Direct messages only between companions or accepted salams
  • Shared map page and OG card; "Prayed together N times" on profiles
  • Message report queue in /admin/reports; host can remove members
  • Email and push for new messages, batched, respecting notification settings

Data & routes

conversation (kind gathering or direct), conversation_member, message (text only at launch, 1,000 chars, soft delete).

companion: user_a < user_b, since, source. prayed_together derived from matching check-ins on the same gathering.

Live delivery: one Durable Object per conversation (new binding), D1 keeps history. Polling every 10s is the fallback if we skip DOs at first.

Measure & accept

Goals: message_sent, companion_added, shared_map_viewed.

  1. A non-member gets 404 on a gathering chat; a removed member loses access at once
  2. A direct message to a non-companion is rejected with a clear sentence
  3. After the close time the chat is read-only
12

Hosts & supporters

Where the first revenue comes from. Mosques and Muslim organisations list halaqas, iftars and Qur'an circles for free and pay to put them in front of travellers in their city. Members can become supporters. Finding and joining always stay free.

Flag phase12.hostsNeeds a spec change first
Flow F12Steward claims host→Lists weekly halaqa→Promote £9 / 7 days→Shown on trips overlapping London→Dashboard: views, joins

Scope

  • Activity kinds on gathering: halaqa, iftar, Qur'an circle, walk, volunteering, sport after Jumu'ah
  • Every activity anchors to a salah (before/after) at a place
  • Verified hosts: stewards and vetted organisations
  • Promotions via Stripe Checkout, one labelled slot per list
  • Supporter subscription via Stripe Billing, cosmetic perks only
  • Host analytics: views, joins, joins from promotion

Data & routes

host: id, name, place_id, verified_at, members. promotion: gathering_id, city_id, starts_on, ends_on, amount, stripe_session_id, status.

subscription: user_id, stripe_customer, plan, status, current_period_end. Webhook: /api/v1/stripe/webhook.

New secrets: STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.

Measure & accept

Goals: activity_created, promotion_purchased, supporter_started.

  1. A paid promotion appears first for trips overlapping its city and dates, and is labelled Promoted
  2. A cancelled subscription removes perks at period end
  3. An unverified user cannot create a promoted activity

Decide before building

These change the spec, so they need a yes before the phase starts. The recommendation is what the board assumes.

Spec §1.7 rules out events and payments

"Event listings beyond prayer times, Eid and Taraweeh" and "donations and payments" are out of scope today. Phases 9 to 11 fit because they're about prayers. Phase 12 needs both lines amended.

Recommend: amend §1.7 when phase 12 starts; keep donations out.

Audience needs a gender field

Sisters-only and brothers-only gatherings need users to self-declare. It's sensitive data and never public.

Recommend: optional, asked only when joining a single-gender gathering; never shown on profiles.

Minimum age for social features

Going counts are harmless, but chat and salam requests between adults and minors are not.

Recommend: 18+ declared at first salam or chat; under 18 can join Families gatherings only.

Live chat infrastructure

The app has no Durable Objects yet. Chat works on D1 with polling but feels slow.

Recommend: ship phase 11 on polling behind the flag, add a Durable Object per conversation once volume justifies it.

Tab bar change

Phase 9 adds Together as a fifth tab. Phase 11 swaps Saved for Inbox and moves Saved into Profile.

Recommend: accept; Saved is still one tap from Profile and the mosque page.

Moderation load

Chat, salam requests and hosted prayers all create new report types.

Recommend: extend /admin/reports with gathering, message and user targets in phase 9, before chat exists.
